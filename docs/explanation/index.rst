Explanation
===========

Deep dives into the concepts, design decisions, and theory behind Katharos.

**Explanations are understanding-oriented.** They clarify and illuminate particular topics. They deepen your knowledge and understanding of Katharos and functional programming.

What You'll Find Here
---------------------

These articles explain:

- **Why** things are designed the way they are
- **How** different concepts relate to each other
- **When** to use different abstractions
- The **mathematical foundations** behind the library
- **Comparisons** with other approaches and libraries

These are not tutorials or how-to guides. They won't teach you how to accomplish specific tasks, but they will help you understand the bigger picture and make better decisions when using Katharos.
